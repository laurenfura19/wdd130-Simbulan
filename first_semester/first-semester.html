Build a routine you can keep

After the first week, focus on managing the work. Keep your routine simple enough for a busy week, and adjust it as you learn what your classes require.

Study with a plan

Check deadlines each week and plan when to work on them. Break big assignments into smaller tasks: choosing a topic, making an outline, and writing a draft. Start early enough to ask questions.

Choose a goal for each study session and put distractions away. Test yourself with practice questions, explain an idea in your own words, or work through an example. Write down specific questions to bring to class, tutoring, or office hours.

Keep track of your money

List the money available for the month, then fixed expenses such as rent and changing costs such as groceries and transportation. Include school supplies and room for unexpected costs when possible.

Record purchases in a notebook, spreadsheet, or app. Review spending weekly and adjust your next week if costs are higher than planned.

Know where to find support

For BYU-Idaho students, these official resources are useful starting points:

  • Academic Success and Tutoring — Find tutoring and learning support.
  • McKay Library — Find research tools and librarian help.
  • Financial Aid — Read assistance information and ask the office about your situation.
  • Student Activities — Explore activities and ways to meet people.

Stay connected

Choose a recurring study group, activity, or service opportunity you enjoy. Make room for rest and people you care about. Once a week, choose one manageable change to improve your routine.
